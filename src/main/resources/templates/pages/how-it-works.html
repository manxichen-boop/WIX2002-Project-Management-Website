How it works

Free, simple and safe exchanges between students

Eco Swape connects students on the same campus so useful things are reused instead of thrown away. Nothing is ever bought or sold.

  1. 01

    Join and get approved

    Register with your university e-mail and confirm it. An administrator approves new accounts so the community stays campus-only.

  2. 02

    Post with photos

    Choose Swap, Giveaway or Upcycling, add 1–6 photos, the condition and a pickup spot. An admin reviews it before it appears in Explore.

  3. 03

    Request and accept

    For swaps you offer one of your approved items. When the owner accepts, both items are reserved and you can see each other's e-mail.

  4. 04

    Hand over and confirm

    Meet at the pickup location. Both of you confirm the handover, and the exchange counts towards campus impact.

Swap

One item for another. The requester must offer an approved, available item of their own.

Giveaway

Free to a good home. Just send a request with a short message.

Upcycling

Cardboard, glass jars, fabric and other materials for creative projects.

Community guidelines

By joining Eco Swape you agree to keep the exchange free, honest and safe:

  • Always free. No prices, payments, deposits or "tips". Listings that ask for money are removed.
  • Honest listings. Use your own photos and describe the condition and any flaws accurately.
  • Allowed items only. No weapons, drugs, alcohol, medicines, counterfeit or stolen goods, or anything unsafe.
  • Be reliable. Turn up when you agree to meet. If plans change, withdraw from the exchange with a reason.
  • Meet safely. Hand over items in public places on campus during the day.
  • Respect privacy. Only use contact details to arrange the exchange.
  • Report problems. Use "Report" on a listing or request. Our administrators review every report.

Why accounts and listings are reviewed

Administrators approve every new account and every new or significantly changed listing. Exchanges themselves go directly between students. Admins only step in when something is reported.
