Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my listing visible in Explore yet?
Every new listing is checked by an administrator first, usually within one working day. You can see its status under My Listings. If changes are requested, edit the listing and it is automatically resubmitted.
Can I sell items on Eco Swape?
No. Eco Swape is completely free: items are swapped, given away or shared for upcycling. Listings that ask for money are removed.
How does a swap work?
Request the item and choose one of your own approved items to offer. If the owner accepts, both items are reserved, you can see each other's e-mail, and you meet at the pickup location. Both of you then confirm the handover to complete the exchange.
What if the other student doesn't show up?
Use "Withdraw" on the accepted exchange and give a short reason. The items become available again. If something felt wrong, report the student and our team will look into it.
Who can see my e-mail address?
Only the other student in an accepted exchange, so you can arrange the handover, and the administrators.
