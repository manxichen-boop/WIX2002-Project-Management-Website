How Eco Swape works

From your shelf to someone's next favourite thing

Every account and every listing is checked by our campus team, so the exchange stays safe and trustworthy. It's always free.

Read the full guide
  1. 01

    Join with your university e-mail

    Register in a minute. An admin approves new accounts to keep the community campus-only.

  2. 02

    List what you no longer need

    Add photos, condition and a pickup spot. Listings go live once they're approved.

  3. 03

    Request and agree

    Ask for an item, offering one of yours for a swap. The owner accepts or declines.

  4. 04

    Meet on campus

    Hand over at the pickup spot, then both confirm. The swap counts towards campus impact.
