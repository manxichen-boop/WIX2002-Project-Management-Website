How we count

  • Completed exchanges are requests where both students confirmed the handover.
  • Items reused counts every item that changed hands in a completed exchange. A swap moves two items, so it counts as two.
  • Recorded weight reused is the sum of the approximate weights owners entered when listing those items (0 of 0 reused items have a recorded weight). Items without a recorded weight add nothing. We don't guess.
  • Approved students are accounts with an active, administrator-approved status.

Why there's no CO₂ figure

Carbon savings depend on what each item would otherwise have been replaced with, and how. We don't have reliable, item-specific emission factors for the things students exchange, so instead of publishing an estimate that looks precise but isn't, we report the verified counts and recorded weight above. If the university adopts an approved conversion methodology, it can be added here and clearly labelled as an estimate.
